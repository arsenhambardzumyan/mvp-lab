Clockify vs Toggl Track: which one fits a small web studio

By Arsen Hambardzumyan, Yerevan. Prices checked 17 November 2026. Last updated 17 November 2026.

Disclosure. The Clockify and Toggl links on this page are currently plain links to the official sites, with no affiliate tracking. I plan to apply to both vendors' affiliate programs; if I join, those links will be replaced with affiliate links and I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This page does not claim hands-on testing: I compared the published pricing pages and FAQs on the date above.

Pick Clockify if your team is 5 people or fewer and you want to stay at $0, or you need paid seats at the lowest per-seat price ($3.99 to $7.99 per user per month on the annual Basic, Standard and Pro plans).

Pick Toggl Track if you are 3 people or fewer on the free plan and its listed features (calendar integrations, focus mode) matter to you. At paid tiers, Toggl's listed prices are higher ($9 and $16 per user per month annual for Starter and Premium).

Where Clockify wins

Price per seat on every listed paid tier, and a free plan limit two users higher. On the Standard plan the pricing page lists invoicing, approvals, time off and a QuickBooks integration; Pro lists budgets, expenses, labor cost and profit. All of this is from the Clockify pricing page.

Where Toggl Track wins

Its pricing page lists Google and Outlook calendar integrations and focus mode on the free plan, and Jira and Asana integrations on Premium. Whether those matter more than the price gap depends on your stack. I have not compared the interfaces and make no claim about which is nicer to use. Source: Toggl Track pricing.

Where each one breaks for a studio

  • Clockify Free limits reports to a 1-month data range and exports to PDF only, so monthly client billing from the free plan means rebuilding reports by hand.
  • Toggl Free stops at 3 users. A fourth person means moving the whole team to Starter: $27 per month at 3 seats on annual billing, $36 at 4 seats (4 x $9).
  • Per-seat pricing: Clockify's FAQ says every owner, admin, manager and member takes a paid seat; check Toggl's terms for how it counts occasional contractors.

FAQ

Which is cheaper for a 5-person studio?

Clockify. Its Free plan allows up to 5 users, and its paid plans at 5 seats cost $19.95 to $39.95 per month on annual billing, against $45 (Starter) or $80 (Premium) for Toggl. Toggl's free plan does not cover 5 users.

Does Toggl Track still have a free plan?

Yes. Its pricing page lists a free plan described as free forever, with a 3-user limit in the FAQ.

How do I move from one to the other?

I have not verified either vendor's import or export steps for this page, so check each vendor's help center before you commit. Clockify's pricing page lists data import and export from the Basic plan, and its Free plan exports PDF only.

When were these prices checked?

17 November 2026, on the two official pricing pages linked above. Prices and limits change, so confirm on the vendor page before buying.
